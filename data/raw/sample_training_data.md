Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run in the park. Every day, Spot chased a big blue ball. One afternoon, the ball rolled under a wooden bench. Spot tried to reach it with his paw, but his leg was too short. A friendly cat named Whiskers walked over. Whiskers reached under the bench and pushed the ball back to Spot. Spot barked happily and shared his favorite chew toy with Whiskers.

Timmy had a red toy car. He loved to push it down the hallway. Zoom, went the little red car! One day, the car lost a wheel. Timmy was very sad. His big sister Sarah saw him crying. "Don't worry, Timmy," Sarah said. She picked up the small wheel and pressed it back onto the car until it clicked. Timmy smiled and gave Sarah a big hug. They played with the car together for the rest of the afternoon.

A small bird named Pip wanted to fly to the top of the big oak tree. His wings were tiny, and he was afraid. "You can do it, Pip," sang his mother from a high branch. Pip took a deep breath, flapped his wings as fast as he could, and flew up into the air. He landed safely on a soft green leaf next to his mother. Pip felt very brave and happy.

Lily found a shiny yellow leaf on the ground. It was smooth and bright. "Look, Mom!" Lily shouted. She showed the leaf to her mother. "It is beautiful," her mother said. They took the leaf home and placed it inside a heavy book to press it flat. Now, Lily keeps the shiny yellow leaf on her desk to remind her of autumn.

Leo loved to bake cookies with his grandfather. They mixed flour, sugar, and chocolate chips in a large bowl. Grandpa turned on the oven until it was warm and cozy. Leo used a spoon to place round dough balls onto the tray. When the cookies were ready, the kitchen smelled wonderful. They drank cold milk and ate warm cookies together by the window.
